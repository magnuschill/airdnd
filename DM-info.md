## Dungeons
We are theming our dungeons loosely around Shambhala stages

* Amphitheatre of the gods - A older style dungeon crawler with a focus on dynamic combat
* The Grove - or rather the Labrinth - focus on puzzles
* The Living Room - A hotel with a focus on social encounters
* The Fractured Forest - Illusions and dealing with the erratic behavior of fae creatures. 
* Pagoda Stronghold - Final Dungeon
* Pandemonium Village - A pocket dimension players are pulled into for the final boss

## Gameplay
The game will be paper, pencil, and dice encouraged. Players can use a digital character sheet, but encourage them to roll dice. We will do theatre of mind - no minis or maps. Let's favor players to just use their actions in a simple way and not worry too much about wargaming strategy. Suggestion: instead of distances in feet just be qualitative like "you can fight them this turn, or you can run up this turn and fight next turn".

There will be a lot of first timers or only minimal experience. Lets make this inviting for them - we can play loosely with the rules and make situations like skill checks simple. Encourage "play by intent" just ask what they want to do and weave it into the narrative. 

### Special Mechanics
* Rest - In every session, a player group can take one short rest and one long rest only. (maybe just short rest in final?)
    * Hit Dice - Only one short rest so just use them all
* Ammunition - consider it unlimited. No magical ammunition?
* Encumberance - Not tracked, each group has a bag of holding (see below)
* Identifying - Players are sent in with many identify scrolls which they can easily use out of combat
* Attunement - Attuning to an item is instant?, un-attuning requires the short rest. Free between sessions
    * Dont even bring it up except for experienced players
* Light - Use at your discretion but keep new player friendly. Ex: "In this area its dark so you either need to remove your shield and hold a torch or your attacks will have disadvantage"
* Death - second failure triggers removal from dungeon so they don't have to re-roll character. In the final dungeon death triggers that player to swap with someone else from their faction from outside the dugeon

### Bag of holding and Equipment
The faction group is always sent in with a bag of holding. They must specifically designate who is holding it (token?) - this is a risk because it could be stolen or that player might become separated.

Players only have their equipped items on their person and 1 (allow 2?) potion. All their other items are in the bag of holding and it takes a full action to retrieve something from the bag.

Players can equip one utility item - ball bearings, climbing gear, etc for quick use.

> Maybe they just have 2 belt "slots" for potions, utility item, scroll, etc.

### Potions
Flavor: potions must be stored in glass or they degrade. You can only equip one(allow 2?) on your belt safely at a time. Drinking your own potion is a bonus action. Administering your potion to another player is your action.

## Sessions
Sessions are 2 hours, strictly. Start on time and end right on time (Ex: Noon start, at 2pm all players are instantly teleported out of dugneon). Make sure the players know the limit and the more decisive and efficient they are the more treasure they will get. At your discretion you can have a 15 minute pre-session for players.

After the session players level up. See if any players need direct help from you or if there is someone in their faction that can help them. Players can roll their HP (pretty fun usually) or just take the average - Let them reroll 1s once. After the first session explain to them that there will be one year in game until the dungeons open again and they can choose an activity and benefit from it (will provide a handout).
