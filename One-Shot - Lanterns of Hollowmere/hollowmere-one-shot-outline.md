# The Lantern at Hollowmere
*A gothic one-shot "Session 0+" for one level 2 character joining a larger campaign. D&D 5e (2014 rules / SRD). Mostly theater of the mind, with the rest of the party handled abstractly.*

---

## 1. Primer

Hollowmere is a fog-choked river town climbing a plague-scarred hill. Its streets are narrow, its physicians wear beaked leather, and every night the Lamplighters burn blue-white vigil lamps along every lane. Folk say the lamps keep "the Quiet" away: the dreamless sleep that sends people walking.

The tone is gothic and spooky, in the spirit of Yharnam: fog, lamplight, whispered rumors, and a medicine that is too good to be true. The horror is seductive, not gory. The monster courts people instead of hunting them.

Underneath the town's old Mill sleeps **the Dreamer**, a charm-based fey-fiend (a succubus/siren type) bound by runes. Her perfumed breath rises through the sewers as the fog. The physicians have been bleeding her for years and selling her blood as **the Draught**, a pale cure-all. After a plague season of record sales, thousands of people carry a faint trace of her. Each one is a **tether**, and each tether feeds her. She is close to breaking free.

The one person who kept her sealed was **Kathra Ironquill**, a Rune Mage who tended the ward-circles at Greywake Abbey. Kathra died in last season's plague, no one maintained the wards, and her ghost now walks the town. She cannot speak: she wails and gestures, and nobody knows who she was. Her cold snuffs lamps wherever she passes, and the Quiet has worsened since she appeared, so everyone blames the haunting. That's the red herring. The real cause is the fog, compounded by the neglected wards.

**Pacing intent:** Three days of investigation, mostly broad scenes and choices with only a little micro-detail. Then Night 3, where everything kicks off, the PC chooses a path, and the one-shot runs a classic five-room delve to a boss fight.

---

## 2. The Truth (DM Eyes Only)

- **The Dreamer** is held beneath the Mill in a rune-bound chamber. Her scent is the fog. Her blood is the Draught.
- **The mist** seeps up from the sewers. Lamps burn it away. Where Kathra's cold snuffs the lamps, the mist creeps into those streets.
- **How the walking works:** Lamps fail along Kathra's route, mist reaches those streets, and Draught users there catch the scent and rise. They drift toward the strongest source of the scent, even a mile from home, and gather near the drains and the Mill.
- **Flame burns the mist away**, which is why the vigil lamps work and why failing lamps are dangerous.
- **Tethers fade.** A dose leaves a trace for roughly a week or two. One serious use is harmless. Habitual use keeps you reachable.
- **Why now:** A plague season drove huge Draught sales, creating many fresh tethers and powering the Dreamer toward breaking her bonds. She needs followers to regain strength.
- **Why Kathra is a red herring:** She really is haunting the town, but she is trying to warn it, not harm it. Her cold snuffs flames, so the lamp failures trace her path. But where the walkers are found (the drains and the Mill) points at the true source.
- **The Quiet predates Kathra.** A little has always existed. It worsened after she appeared, and the town links the two, but the real cause is the Dreamer's growing strength and the neglected ward.
- **Only Kathra's blessing can truly banish the Dreamer** to the Feywild. Without it she is vanquished but not gone.
- **If Kathra is simply killed**, the lamps hold, the town relaxes, and the seals fail about a month later.

## 3. World Rules

### The Tether Check
Anyone who has taken Draught in roughly the last one to two weeks and catches any whiff of the mist makes a **Wisdom save** (suggested DC 11-12). On a failure they sleepwalk toward the Mill. This is a roll, not a certainty, so not every Draught user walks and not every walker used it recently. The link is real but indirect, and the PC still has to find the mist connection.

### Lamps and Mist
Vigil lamps burn away the mist in a short radius. Where lamps fail, the mist thickens and the tether check applies.

### The Lesser Charm (on the PC)
The thralls are fully enchanted. The PC faces a lesser version: impactful but not loss of control. Suggested: disadvantage on attacks against the Dreamer, or hesitation before targeting her. Details to be finalized in the encounters phase.

### The Rest of the Party
The party holds the bridge and the streets. Their fights resolve with a single pass/fail roll. In the dungeon they can handle most of a large group (for example, six enemies where the PC only has to fight two).

### Clues Find the Player
Every clue arrives through a scene event (a letter, an overheard argument, a ledger), so the PC gets the whole story even without a roll. Rolls add detail and extras.

## 5. People

### Kathra Ironquill (ghost) | dwarf

*Look:* Iron-grey braids, chalk dust on her fingers, a pale blue glow, and a chill that snuffs flames.

A dwarven Rune Mage and the abbey's ward-warden. She wasn't a priest, and her power was rune-work. In her later years she was a hermit who came into town only for food and said little. She is tied to the abbey by unfinished business (the wards), wanders the streets and her old cottage, and died in last season's plague and is buried in the cemetery next to the abbey. Her runework is etched into the vigil-lamp glass, though the town only ever called her "the Warden." She cannot speak: she wails and gestures. Speaking her **name** calms her. She is tragic, not evil.

### Wren Cade (main ally) | human

*Look:* Lean, soot-streaked hands, and a hitch in his walk he tries to hide.

- A journeyman Lamplighter in his thirties.
- **Injury:** A fall from a lamp scaffold years ago shattered his hip, right outside the old drain grate near Cinder Row. The physicians prescribed Draught for the pain.
- **History:** He has been off the Draught for about two years, but damp and long days flare the injury.
- **Family:** His younger sister **Isla** (human, a seamstress and a regular Draught user). His mother taught him the lamps.
- **Why he asks for help:** Isla is slipping and the constable won't act.
- **What he leaves out:** His letter never names the Draught. He is covering for Isla's overuse and his own history.
- **Payment:** His late mother's **lantern**, an heirloom that becomes the PC's magic item.
- **Arc:** After a rough 48 hours of walking and fighting alongside the PC, his hip flares on Day 3. He slips and pockets or takes a vial. Even if the party confiscates it, he wakes at midnight and points out the sleepwalkers outside, which is what spurs the party into action.
- **Romance option** with the PC if the player wants it.

### Dr. Lenore Ashbury (apothecary and lead physician) | human

*Look:* Tall, spotless gloves, and a faint sweet scent, the same as the fog (a subtle tell).

Charming, defensive, and professional when the PC meets her on Day 2. She is secretly a thrall (there may be others) and is "on blood duty" on Night 3, which is how she ends up in the finale. A villain-but-not: she is tragic rather than purely evil.

### Constable Dench | half-orc

*Look:* Broad, filed-down tusks, a rumpled coat, and he rubs his jaw while he thinks.

Weary, sidelined, half-paid. He knows more than he admits and won't act without proof. Rarely seen outside Cinder Row; elsewhere only if sought out.

### Mother Bree Goodbarrel (innkeeper) | halfling

*Look:* Round, flour-dusted, and points with a ladle. Calls everyone "lamb."

Warm, nosy, and knows everyone's business. Runs the Gutterwick Inn. A source of rumors and local color.

### Tamsin Reed (barmaid) | half-elf

*Look:* Lanky, ink on her fingers from writing orders, quick with a joke, always yawning.

Works the Inn's late shift and sees the street from the window. She can mention the sleepwalkers passing by on Night 3.

### Phelaia (Sallow Court fence) | tiefling

*Look:* Violet skin, brass-ringed horns, and she rolls a coin over her knuckles.

Runs a stall on Cinder Row. Sells cut street Draught. She can be bargained with, bribed, or threatened. Not all Sallow Court members know the full truth, and some will turn on the physicians.

### Fonkin Pennywhistle (the Lightmaster, Master of the Lamplighters) | gnome

*Look:* Swivel-lens spectacles, ink everywhere, and he mutters numbers under his breath.

Fussy, brilliant, and proud of his ledgers. He gives the PC the lamp-failure ledger without being asked and fears the Lamplighters will be blamed.

### Rhogar (gravekeeper) | dragonborn

*Look:* Heavy shoulders, slate-grey scales, and he hums hymns whose words he's forgotten.

Tends Greywake Cemetery. The only one who connected the ghost to a person. He buried Kathra in haste during the plague, without proper rites, and blames himself for her restlessness. He is afraid to speak her name to her, so he has done nothing. He gives her name and shows her grave to anyone who asks or spends time with him, and is the Night 3 fallback.

## 6. Timeline

### Night 0 (offscreen)
Kathra walks. Lamps die. Sleepwalkers drift toward the Mill. The party arrives the next evening.

### Hook
Wren wrote to the party asking for help and offering his mother's lantern. The letter is in `hollowmere-hook-handout.md`. The party and PC arrive together on the river road at dusk and meet Wren at the bridge on the edge of town.

### Arrival Evening (Night 0)
- Meet Wren at the bridge, then a late, after-hours check-in at the **Gutterwick Inn** with just Mother Bree and Wren. The Lamplighters' notice is on the door.
- At most a glimpse of a pale figure and a distant wail.

### Day 1: The Failing Lights
- **Lamplighters' Hall:** Fonkin hands over the ledger, which shows lamp failures tracing a path across town.
- Witnesses describe the walkers, who gather near the sewers and the Mill, and the Draught link appears.
- **Night 1:** Tamsin pours. Nobody knows who the ghost is, but Bree or Tamsin mention an unnamed hermit warden who died in the plague. No pressure to act.

### Day 2: Investigation
- **Ashbury's Apothecary:** Meet the physician. Bottles are labeled with the Mill. Isla is buying a bottle as the PC walks in.
- **Second scene (choose one):** Phelaia on Cinder Row (Dench glimpsed looking the other way) or Kathra's cottage.
- **Night 2:** Quiet. Wren is limping.

### Day 3: The Pieces Come Together
- **Greywake Abbey and Cemetery:** The faded wards, the abbey tunnel, the register, and Rhogar, who gives her name.
- **Evening at the Inn:** If Wren slips, the dose comes from the street, Isla's supply, or Cinder Row, never a second apothecary visit.
- **Night 3:** Lamps fail, Kathra walks, and sleepwalkers drift toward the Mill. Wren wakes at midnight and points them out. Tamsin also sees them pass the Inn windows.

## 8. Night 3: Two Paths

### The Sewers (straightforward, harder)
Take the old drain stairs near Cinder Row down into the sewers. This runs the full five-room dungeon.

### The Ghost (riskier, rewarded)
Meet Kathra as she walks. She cannot speak. She wails and gestures, and she attacks anyone who approaches armed and without her name. The fight is tough upfront, but speaking her **name** (from Rhogar or the abbey register) calms her. If she is put to rest:
- She points out the **abbey tunnel**, skipping rooms 1-3.
- She gives the **blessing**: advantage on saves against the Dreamer's charm, and spirit-light that lets the PC's weapons banish the Dreamer.
- The wards need no repair. Once the Dreamer is banished, the threat is over.

### Dungeon Outline (rooms detailed later)
1. **Fog Drains:** charm hazard and traps. Fire clears the mist.
2. **Sallow Court Gate:** talk, bribe, threaten, or fight.
3. **Cistern Puzzle:** reignite the lamp channels.
4. **Ossuary Stair:** tethered townsfolk. The party handles most of them.
5. **The Dreamer** with Dr. Ashbury (and possibly other physician thralls), with backup from the party or turncoat thieves.

## 9. Endings

1. **"Good?":** The PC destroys Kathra and stops there. Relit lamps hold and the town relaxes, but a month later the seals fail and the Dreamer walks free. (Free campaign hook.)
2. **Hard win ("dead but not gone"):** The PC takes the sewers and vanquishes the Dreamer without Kathra's blessing. An evil laugh follows them out of the sewers. The Quiet continues, and she will return.
3. **True good:** The PC puts Kathra to rest and fights with her blessing. The Dreamer is banished to the Feywild, the Quiet stops, and Kathra passes on knowing someone will carry on.
