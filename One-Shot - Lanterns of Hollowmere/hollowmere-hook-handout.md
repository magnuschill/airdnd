# The Lantern at Hollowmere: Hook Handout

---

## 1. The Letter (player handout)

*Received three days ago. The company wrote back that they would come. Hand this to the PC at the start of the session and let them read it aloud.*

---

> *To whoever among the Emerald Enclave this reaches:*
>
> I'll be plain, since I'm no hand at letters. My name is **Wren Cade**. I'm a journeyman of the Lamplighters, and I keep the vigil lamps along the lower lanes of Hollowmere.
>
> Things are going wrong here. The lamps are going out, late in the night... and the *Quiet* is getting worse. People rise from their beds and walk. *They don't speak. They don't wake.* They wander until someone turns them home.
>
> The constable says it is the damp. The physicians called it *nerves*, handed out calming tea, and lined their pockets. Even the Lightmaster — who *should* know better — says to wait it out.
>
> I would ask you not to judge anything until you've seen it for yourselves — but there are rumors of a white figure where the lamps die... and that the old abbey on the edge of town is restless. I don't know what to make of it.
>
> **I can't wait.** My sister is among the ones who walk. I can offer a reward. It isn't much, but I will honor it.
>
> Come by the river road, and come **before dusk**. I'll meet you at the bridge. The Gutterwick Inn will keep rooms for you; tell *Mother Bree* I sent you. And whatever else you do — **keep a flame on you.**
>
> *— Wren Cade*
> *Journeyman, the Lamplighters*

---

## 2. Arrival (read aloud)

> Three days since the letter, and you wrote back that you'd come. Now the river road drops toward Hollowmere as the light fails. At the bridge on the edge of town, a lean figure waits with a lantern, favoring one leg. He steps forward as he sees you.

---

## 3. Lamplighters' Notice (posted on the Inn door, seen on arrival night)

> **BY ORDER OF THE LAMPLIGHTERS**
>
> Citizens of Hollowmere are advised to keep lamps lit and doors barred from dusk. Should you find a neighbor walking in sleep, **do not wake them.** Turn them gently toward home. The Lamplighters regret any inconvenience.
>
> *— Fonkin Pennywhistle, Lightmaster*

---

## 4. DM Notes

**What the letter plants (the PC has it even without a roll):**
- **The lamps are going out late at night, and the Quiet is worse.** Common town knowledge, with no cause attached.
- **People walk in their sleep until someone turns them home.** The notice on the Inn door echoes this.
- **Nobody is acting.** The constable, the physicians, and even the Lightmaster.
- **A white figure and a restless abbey.** Rumor only. It opens the Ghost path without pushing it.
- **A sister, a reward, a bridge, and a flame.** Personal stakes, a vague payment, a meeting point, and one practical order with no reason given.

**What it deliberately leaves out (the PC finds these):**
- The fog and its sweet smell.
- The Draught. Wren is covering for Isla and for his own history.
- The Mill and the sewers.
- Any link between the lamps, the fog, and the sleepwalkers.

**Tells for Insight:**
- "Lined their pockets" is real anger, but he never names what the physicians sell. He is leaving something out.
- He gives no reason for "keep a flame on you." Ask him at the bridge, and let him say he doesn't fully know, only that it matters.

**Delivering it:** Print on aged or tea-stained paper if you can. Let the PC read it aloud to the party, then cut straight to the bridge.
