# The Lantern at Hollowmere — Combat Quick Reference
*D&D 5e (2014) • Level 2 featured PC (~12 HP) • Three optional fights • Homebrew unless marked SRD*

## Table Rules (All Encounters)
- **Outside the finale:** Focus on a **1v1** for the PC. Other adventurers handle offscreen threats; don't add their turns to initiative.
- **Finale:** The PC chooses **one companion** (Cleric, Warrior, or Mage) to fight alongside them. The rest of the party is occupied offscreen.
- **Potions carried by PC:** 2 × Healing (**2d4+2 HP**) and 1 × Greater Healing (**4d4+4 HP**). **House rule:** drink as a bonus action; administer to another creature as an action.
- **Enemy morale:** Thieves can surrender/flee; Kathra can be talked down. Avoid enemies focusing a downed PC. Allow disengagement and surrender.
- **Optional offscreen party roll:** Once/round, d20 vs **DC 11**: success means allies contain an additional threat; failure means they remain occupied. **No extra damage to the PC on a failure.** In the finale, instead use *one* successful-roll benefit: cancel one enemy attack **or** deal 1d4 to the Dreamer **or** give the PC advantage on their next relevant check. Do not use this roll if it would duplicate the chosen companion's spotlight.

## 1. Kathra's Restless Spirit — Solo Ghost (Optional)
**Setup:** PC vs. Kathra (1v1). Goal: survive, drive her off, **or** resolve her unfinished duty peacefully. *Custom weakened undead; not the SRD Ghost/Specter.*

| AC | HP | Speed | Attack | Save/Ability |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **12** | **14** | Fly 30 ft. | **Chilling Touch:** +3 hit, melee 5 ft., **1d4 cold** | **Warding Pulse (1/fight):** DC 10 WIS; failure = cannot willingly move closer to Kathra until end of next turn; no damage |

**Traits:** Can drift through creatures/objects (cannot end movement inside solid matter). **No weapon resistances** for this weakened form. One action per turn.

**Suggested beats**
1. **Round 1:** Materializes, warns the PC; uses **Warding Pulse** if threatened.
2. **Round 2:** Uses **Chilling Touch** only if attacked or cornered; her words/gestures point toward the failing wards.
3. **Round 3+:** PC fights, retreats, or invokes Kathra's **true name / old lamp / ward journal**. **DC 10 Persuasion or Arcana** can end hostility; compelling evidence/appeal succeeds without a roll. A failed attempt may be retried with a new approach.

**Resolution:** Peacefully put to rest → opens the **Abbey ward-conduit**, explains resealing, grants **Blessing of the Last Vigil**. Reduced to 0 HP → dissipates **without** blessing or ward knowledge.

**Blessing (PC only; through final fight):** **+1d4 to attacks and saving throws** (Bless-like; no concentration), **advantage on saves against Dreamer's charm**, and **knowledge to reseal wards**.

## 2. Sallow Court Cutpurse — Street or Gate (Optional)
**Setup:** Normally **one cutpurse vs. the PC**. If two are present, party allies engage the second; do not have both focus the 12-HP PC. *2014 SRD Bandit stats, renamed.*

| AC | HP | Speed | Melee | Ranged |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **12** | **11** | 30 ft. | **Scimitar:** +3 hit, **1d6+1 slashing** | **Light crossbow:** +3 hit, 80/320 ft., **1d8+1 piercing** |

**Suggested beats**
1. **Round 1:** Threat, demand, or standoff; PC may pay, persuade, intimidate, retreat, or attack.
2. **Round 2:** Cutpurse attacks once; other thieves are held off by the rest of the party if present.
3. **Round 3+:** At **3 HP or less**, cutpurse surrenders or flees when plausible. Phelaia's prior goodwill can end the fight early.

**Resolution:** Gain passage/information through negotiation, victory, or surrender. Avoid a lethal fight over a minor toll.

## 3. The Bound Dreamer + Dr. Ashbury — Final Boss (2v2)
**Setup:** **PC + chosen companion** vs. **Dreamer + Ashbury**. The Dreamer is a succubus-flavored fey-fiend **severely weakened by her bindings**; these are custom encounter stats, **not** the full-strength CR 4 Succubus. Remaining allies are busy with thralls.

| Enemy | AC | HP | Action / Damage | Special |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Bound Dreamer** | **12** | **24** | **Dreamer's Touch:** +3 hit, melee 5 ft., **1d4 psychic** | **Beguiling Whisper (recharge 5–6):** *action*, target within 30 ft. DC 11 WIS; failed save → disadvantage on next attack against Dreamer. **No Multi attack - this uses an Action.**  <br>- Dreamer Cannot leave chamber while bound. |
| **Dr. Ashbury** | **11** | **8** | **Surgical Knife:** +3 hit, melee 5 ft., **1d4+1 piercing** | **Administer Draught (1/fight):** *action*, restore **1d4 HP** to Dreamer instead of attacking. |

**Suggested beats**
1. **Round 1 — Pressure:** Dreamer speaks/uses **Beguiling Whisper**; Ashbury moves to block or attacks. Companion acts normally.
2. **Round 2 — Complication:** Dreamer uses **Touch**; Ashbury may expend her **Draught** to heal. PC can attack, drink a potion, protect an ally, or work on the wards.
3. **Round 3+ — Decision:** PC defeats Ashbury, attacks Dreamer, or restores seals with Kathra's knowledge. Allies hold back other threats; boss cannot use charm and attack in the same action.

**Alternate win — Banished :** If blessed by Kathra's Spirit, and attacks made with Bless +1d4, lethal damage will Only Kathra's blessing (holy / blessed damage) can truly banish her to the Feywild. Without it she can be defeated, but not gone. (PC leaves the room, hears a faint evil snicker)

**Possible outcomes:** **True victory:** Kathra helped, seals restored. \
 **Hard victory:** Dreamer defeated, seals unrepaired. \
**False victory:** Ghost destroyed/ignored and Dreamer left active; she eventually escapes.\
**Future rematch:** Unbound Dreamer returns at approximately **full Succubus strength (CR 4, 66 HP)**—*not* a Level 2 fight.

## Chosen Companion — Quick Stats (Finale Only)
*Simplified homebrew allies; each takes one turn per round. No full character sheets required.*

| Companion | AC | HP | Action | Support |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Cleric** | **15** | **14** | **Mace:** +4 hit, **1d6+2 bludgeoning** | **Healing Word (2 uses):** bonus action, ally within 60 ft. heals **1d4+2 HP**. May attack with mace on same turn (table simplification). |
| **Warrior** | **16** | **18** | **Longsword:** +4 hit, **1d8+2 slashing** | **Bodyguard (1/fight):** reaction when PC is hit; Warrior takes that attack instead (same damage roll). |
| **Mage** | **12** | **10** | **Arcane Bolt:** +4 hit, range 60 ft., **1d6+2 force** | **Disrupt Magic (1/fight):** action, end an active Dreamer charm effect on an ally **or** prevent Ashbury's next healing action. |

**DM survival checks:** Keep incoming enemy damage low; don't stack both enemies' attacks on the PC when avoidable. A companion may stabilize a downed PC or administer a potion using their action. Pause to allow a short rest before the finale if the fiction permits. **Balance target:** brief, tense, recoverable—not guaranteed safe; adjust if actual PC AC/damage differs substantially.
