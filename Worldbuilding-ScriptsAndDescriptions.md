# Descriptions & Worldbuilding

## Individual dungeons

Within each dungeon it's likely there is some similar architecture or design elements that do make it clear it's a part of the whole... But how can you accomplish this when each individual dungeon is so different? And what are the different themes looking to be pulled together, that help distinguish each dungeon so differently?

It's possible to replace the items in [brackets] to help personalize your dungeon but similar descriptions or patterns of details help to draw the whole thing together and be more cohesive
:
> "As you enter the space, you notice somewhere [on the floor], a strange, indented portion. You move to investigate it closer, [clearing away some overgrown greenery, vines and creepers] and discover there's actually a recessed niche, [like that of a gothic cathedral] - has been carved into the stonework [floor]. Its the space where you might expect to find a statue, or painting, (or indeed - a stained glass window) - but strangely here it's empty.\
>The space itself is roughly 6-8 feet long, and a couple of feet wide, [it's large enough to accommodate a life-size statue of a standard medium sized creature]. The material is [stone], and feels cool to the touch, and looks somewhat weathered or aged along the edges. [You notice where small pieces of stone have maybe chipped off - it's clearly old.] \
>Last thing you notice, right along the edge where the wall of this recess meets the floor you stand on, some of the decorative trim has this metallic strip of a clean metal, that borders all around the archway. It appears still well preserved or otherwise untouched by the elements - and the color and shine reminds you of platinum or a similar precious metal.\
>As you finally stand up from looking at the indentation, you notice that scattered [around the room or across the floor in a pattern] are more of these little alcoves, for a total of [5].

If these alcoves looked similar to the Epic Dungeon shown to the players by the Lords Alliance, when first issued the quest to pursue the 5 Keys, and tied back to the main temple/ancient castle/etc., it becomes a clear indication that the group is on the right track, and this is in fact the way to the next dungeon they seek.

Could ALSO then tie in the individual dungeon elements when they use these to access the dungeon:
>Laying down in the alcove, and settle in for a moment as you lay still, you feel as [water] begins to spill into and pool up around you, coming from - nowhere?... In fact, on closer inspection, it's actually pouring out from that platinum border that runs all around the niche, some enchantment within producing the water from nothing.\
>If you allow it to raise up around you, and remain in the outcropping... you start to feel light, buoyant almost, as if you were floating in the ocean. But the feeling is, slightly off... the direction isn't right, and almost like it's pulling you _into_ the [floor].\
>As you let it - you seem to drift beneath the surface, holding your breath, and you watch as the world in front of you darkens... You blink a few times and that darkness is gone, instead you see river rocks and small specks of dirt floating in water. you can feel your back is now exposed to the open air, and as you lift your head - you are, in fact, floating on your stomach in the middle of a [small creek or stream or fountain etc].

Back at the main castle/location, these each need a bit more... detail.

>Led into the room, you see a grand hall like setting, with columns and supports, a massively tall ceiling, and at the far end, next to several tents and temporary shelters set up, [five] large marbled arches, carved outcroppings in beautiful and old stone.\
>On closer inspection, these niches are only carved about a foot deep into the wall, and just about centered in the arch, is a slightly larger that average keyhole, and around it is a continuous bead of platinum trim that outlines and stands out from the rest of the stonework.\
>Inside the key hole itself is dark, smooth stone - it looks like it has been carved out, but just into the existing rock. Looking in with a torch nearby, it doesn't give way to another room on the other side, or anything you can see at all - no mechanism, no pins or locks to be seen, simply an indent or recess within the arch itself.

After collecting multiple different keys, it's possible to provide this piece of dialogue: 

>There seems to be this magnetic like pull between each of the keys. Weak, but definitely noticeable - especially when you hold one or two it in hand, or can see another one nearby.\
>If you set two new keys on a table - (it doesn't launch itself like a strong magnet, more like turns towards it slightly like a compass).\
>If you place the key into one of the archways in the Grand Hall, you notice the size does fit well, it slides in and you can feel it reach the stone wall and bottom out, but as you turn the handle it doesn't rotate or move. It looks like the right fit, just maybe not the right key.

When all 5 keys are put together or held in hand by one person; 
>As you put the 5 keys next to each other - you notice they start to almost meld or phase together, like the shapes actually overlapping and sharing the same physical space. As each new key connects, small arcane sparks fly and sizzle as the multiple shapes seem to orient themselves and wobble and turn and shimmer like a mirage.\
>The movement starts to settle down, and the features of each different key is now noticeable, all together, like pieces of a puzzle fitting into the gaps or void left by the others.\
>The resulting key then starts to shudder and shake slightly before diffusing and splitting into 5 now identical, platinum colored keys. It's almost as if that same strange force that pulled them in before merging them, was suddenly inverted and forced the objects out from occupying the same physical space in this plane. You are now in possession of 5 identical Platinum Keys.

And in turn, these Platinum Keys can actually be used properly to open the doors or passageways in the Great Hall via the keyholes. These new combined keys now WILL turn, opening either a portal of light or something, and after entering the key is consumed or mystically dissolves in the lock, or the portal closes behind the person, and only works if all 5 keys are turned at once (one person per archway, one person through per key.)
